# Mill direction fix — items for the owner to confirm

> **Status: not yet run on the real database.** This file is regenerated by
> `pnpm fix:mill-direction` (dry run) against a **copy** of the production database. Until then it
> shows the format with a worked example from the test fixture (`tests/sync/fix-mill-direction.test.ts`).

## How to use it

1. Copy the database: `mongodump --uri=<prod>` then `mongorestore --uri=<copy> --nsFrom='<db>.*' --nsTo='<db>_fixcopy.*'`.
2. Dry run on the copy: `pnpm fix:mill-direction --db=<db>_fixcopy`. It writes this file,
   `docs/MILL_FIX_RECONCILIATION.md` and a template `docs/mill-fix-decisions.json`.
3. Fill in `docs/mill-fix-decisions.json` (keys are document ids, listed in the last column below):
   - `payments`: `"IN"` = this was money **from** the mill (it was entered the wrong way round; cash
     or bank goes up by 2 × the amount) or `"REFUND"` = you really paid the mill (kept as a refund to
     the mill, no cash change).
   - `rates`: for a v2.0 "for milling" dispatch, the **price per quintal the mill pays**, in rupees
     (`"2600"`). The old rate was a milling charge, so the script never guesses it.
   - `credits`: for a paddy return that had a v2.0 "stock value", the credit to the mill in rupees,
     or `"DEFAULT"` for quantity × the dispatch rate.
4. Run again on the copy with `--apply`, check `docs/MILL_FIX_RECONCILIATION.md`, then run on
   production with `--apply`. Every run is idempotent.

A Gola with an undecided **blocking** item is left completely unchanged.

## Worked example (test fixture, dry run)

### Payments marked as paid TO a mill

| Gola | Date | No. | Mill | Amount | Now | Proposed | Blocking | Decision (id) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rampur | 2026-11-11 | PAY-000003 | Mill One | ₹1000.00 | OUT (CASH) — counted as money paid to the mill | IN if this was money FROM the mill (cash/bank +2× amount); REFUND if you really paid the mill | yes | — (`d000003`) |

### Dispatches that stored a milling charge, not a price

| Gola | Date | No. | Mill | Amount | Now | Proposed | Blocking | Decision (id) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rampur | 2026-11-10 | MIL-000002 | Mill One | ₹1000.00 | milling charge 5000 paise/qtl (total 100000 paise, was payable TO the mill) | enter the price per quintal the mill pays | yes | — (`d000002`) |

### Paddy returns with an old value

None.

### Consignment sales that need a manual settlement

None. (A single consignment sale with an exact total is converted automatically; two or more on
one dispatch, or a rounded total, would be listed here.)

### Sales from paddy lying at a mill (information)

None.
